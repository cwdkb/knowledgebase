Inbound Call Handling (Call Center & Lead Management Clerk)

The full call flow from greeting to close — use this for any inbound call, new lead or existing client.

1. Greeting & Introduction

Script: "Good [morning/afternoon], thank you for calling Complete Windows & Doors, this is [Your Name], how can I help you today?"

☐ Answer within 3 rings. ☐ Smile before answering (tone carries). ☐ Introduce self + company. ☐ Offer help immediately.

2. Identify the Caller's Need

Script (Inbound Lead): "Great, I can help with that. May I start with your name and best contact number?" · "What type of project are you considering — windows, doors, or both?" · "Is this for replacement, new construction, or repair?" · "Do you have a timeline in mind?"

Script (Service / Existing Client): "Can I get your project or invoice number so I can look up your file?" · "Can you describe the issue you're experiencing?"

☐ Capture full name, phone, email, and address. ☐ Identify project type. ☐ Confirm timeline. ☐ Note lead source (Google, referral, ad, etc.).

3. Qualify the Lead

Script: "To make sure we connect you with the right specialist, can I ask a few quick questions?" · "How many windows/doors are you looking to replace?" · "Are you interested in standard replacements, custom options, or energy-efficient upgrades?" · "Do you already have a budget range in mind?"

☐ Enter answers in Builder Prime. ☐ Mark lead status (New / Qualified / Needs Follow-up). ☐ Tag lead source.

4. Set Next Steps

Script (Booking Appointment): "The next step is to schedule a free consultation with one of our specialists. We have availability on [date/time] — would that work for you?" · "Perfect. I'll confirm your appointment for [date/time]. You'll receive a reminder by [call/text/email]."

Script (Info Request Only): "I'll send you an email with our product guide and contact information so you can review before booking." · "Would you like me to follow up in a few days?"

☐ Confirm date/time. ☐ Assign to correct sales rep. ☐ Send confirmation. ☐ Set CRM follow-up reminder.

5. Closing the Call

Script: "Thank you, [Customer Name], we look forward to helping with your window and door project. If you need anything before your appointment, feel free to call us at [phone number]. Have a great day!"

☐ Repeat name, date, time. ☐ Thank caller by name. ☐ End-of-day: review missed leads and schedule follow-ups.

Lead Intake / Quote Request Follow-Up

For a customer who's already reached out asking for a quote — booking the appointment and handling the one common non-fit case (screen repair only).

Booking Script

"Hi [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from Complete Windows & Doors. We appreciate your interest in getting a quote from our company! I have appointment openings available on [date options]. Please let me know which date and time works best for you."

Then confirm: "Can you please let us know what products you're interested in, so that we may best assist you?" and "Can you please provide your full property address?"

"Great! Thanks so much for speaking with me today. You should see confirmation of the appointment shortly. We look forward to seeing you soon!"

Referral Case — Screen Repair Only (Not a Fit)

"For window screen repair services, we recommend contacting Glass Doctor of Barrie. They specialize in glass and screen repair and replacement, and we often refer our customers to them. If you mention that Complete Windows & Doors sent you, they'll take good care of you and provide a quote. You can reach them at 705-721-4527 or visit their website at www.glassdoctor.com/barrie."

Branch-specific — confirm whether this referral partner still applies, or if it needs a local equivalent per branch/region.

Post-Estimate Follow-Up Call (CEI)

Called a few days after a sales rep's estimate appointment — checks satisfaction (Customer Experience Index) and moves the client toward a decision. This is the newer, "integrated" version that folds structured CEI scoring into a warm conversational call — see Open Questions for a note on an older duplicate draft.

Greeting & Purpose

"Hello [Client's Name], this is [Your Name] from Complete Windows & Doors. How are you today?"

"I'm calling to follow up on your recent experience/estimate appointment with our sales representative, [Sales Rep's Name], that took place [this date/week/month]. We are always looking for ways to better improve, and wanted to ensure you feel completely comfortable and informed before moving forward. Is now an okay time, or I can call back this evening/tomorrow/later?"

1. Experience

"On a scale of 1 to 10, how satisfied were you with your initial consultation? Did [Sales Rep's Name] provide all the information you needed and answer your questions thoroughly?"

If yes: "That's wonderful to hear! We're glad you had a positive experience."

If no: "I'm sorry to hear that. Could you please let me know what could have been improved? We're always looking to enhance our services."

2. Customer Service

"Were you satisfied with the response time of your sales rep during the process? Was the time between your estimate appointment and receiving your formal quote reasonable and communicated clearly?"

3. Communication

"Were you kept informed at every stage of the process? How would you rate the attitude and professionalism of your sales rep?"

4. Recommendation

"Would you feel confident recommending your sales rep and Complete Windows & Doors to friends or family? Are there any questions or concerns you'd like us to address regarding the estimate, products, or installation process?"

5. Next Steps & Closing

"Is there anything else you need from us to help you move forward with your decision? Would you like to schedule a follow-up appointment to revisit the quote, either in person, over the phone, or via email?"

"Thank you so much for your time, [Client's Name]. We appreciate your feedback and look forward to the opportunity to work with you. If you feel ready to proceed or if there are any updates regarding your project, please don't hesitate to let us know. We're here to assist you every step of the way. Have a great day!"

Notes

Listen attentively and take notes. Escalate any concerns to the appropriate team member immediately. Maintain a friendly, professional tone throughout.
