Daily & Year-End Checklists

Structure and consistency so no key admin duties get missed.

Admin Staff Daily Checklist

Morning: disarm alarm, lights/music on, check voicemails/emails/SMS boards, review to-do list, check lead sources from overnight, address new leads in CRM.

Midday: work emails in priority order (client comms → lead follow-ups 2nd-4th touch → general admin), forward invoices to AP, apply AR payments, prep contracts, re-check lead buckets.

End of day: recheck SMS/emails, tidy to-do list, review/organize lead & contract buckets, charge credit card machine, shred sensitive docs, secure warehouse/garage, arm alarm and lock up.

Year-End Admin Staff Checklist

Email & comms: prep Gift of Family / Top Choice Vote campaign lists, send to Marketing.

Excel & tracking: new-year Referral Tracker, Deposit Tracker, Commission Cards, Payroll Tracker; archive prior-year versions; save shared Teams docs to company files.

Filing: move prior-year client files to archive storage, file current year's docs, pick a new file colour for the upcoming year.

Office/supplies: clean and organize supplies + warehouse storage, restock consumables, clean up digital folders/duplicates.

Office deep clean: schedule deep clean, polish display units, dispose of shredding.

Seasonal decor: take down and store Christmas decor.

Holiday closure: update voicemail, Google business hours, website hours, and Out of Office for the info inbox; remind team to set OOO.

Year-end financial/AR: call AR clients in December for outstanding balances, review prior-year commission report for red jobs.

Year-End Forms Reset (Microsoft Teams)

Resets Microsoft Forms at year-end without recreating them, preserving permissions/structure so responses keep syncing to shared Excel files (not personal folders).

Key rules: Forms must stay linked to an unlocked channel to sync; locked channels can't host Forms/synced workbooks; never delete column headers in a synced Excel file.

Process: archive prior-year Excel data → delete the old synced workbook → rename the Form for the new year → replace the Form link/tab in the locked channel → create a new synced workbook → clear only data rows (keep headers).

Office & Systems

Mailbox handling, email confirmations, and the M365 / phone system admin tools.

General Mailbox Legacy — HCP

This doc is from 2023 and refers to HCP (HouseCall Pro) throughout — the company now runs on Builder Prime. Treat the workflow shape as directional only until re-confirmed.

Telephone: check main voicemail (password on file), collect name/number/details from messages.

Email: flag anything awaiting response; if unanswered half a day, follow up on ownership; acknowledge receipt if no one can respond yet; file under Customer email folder once resolved.

Leads: collect name/phone/address/product/how they heard about us/best time to quote; check repeat-client info; book with same sales associate if repeat; otherwise next available.

Invoices, confirmations, signed contracts, EMT payments, service, Google/social: each has its own file-naming and filing convention — see full doc for the exact steps.

Email Inbox Confirmations

Forward confirmation to Production (Steve D. or Gerry C.) → upload the Confirmation PDF into the client's Documents tab in Builder Prime, into the "Order Confirmation" folder → file the email under the Confirmations folder in the inbox.

If a newer confirmation replaces an older one for the same product, delete the old one so the folder only holds the latest version.

Clock In & Clock Out (Builder Prime)

Installer time tracking — warehouse and jobsite.

Installer Clock In/Out Process

Purpose: accurately record installer hours at warehouse and jobsite for payroll and job costing.

Heading straight to a job: Clock into Work & Job at the warehouse.

Warehouse work first: Clock into Work & Admin Task (General Labour) at the warehouse.

Warehouse → job: clock out of Admin Task, then clock into the job.

Job → warehouse: clock out of Job, clock into Admin Task; clock out of Work at end of day.

Switching jobs: enter completion notes/photos at the first job → clock out of Job → clock into the next job on arrival.

Finishing a job: enter completion notes/photos, hand in paperwork to Production, confirm everything's logged in BP, then clock out of Job and Work.

Clock In/Out Quick Reminder

Loading & heading to a job → Clock into Job & Work at the warehouse.

Doing warehouse work → Clock into Admin Task at the warehouse.

Final clock out (end of day) → Clock out of Work at the warehouse.

Bookkeeping & Payments

Accounts receivable/payable flow, payroll, and payment recording.

Bookkeeping Flow Chart & Process

Accounts Receivable: deposits mostly by credit/e-transfer (some EFT/cheque/cash); all payments printed, recorded in the CRM and client file, then manually recorded in Sage. Cash/cheques go on a weekly deposit sheet before banking.

Accounts Payable: invoices arrive by email, printed, and (for stock/equipment/siding/hardware) routed to Production for approval before recording. Window/door manufacturer invoices skip approval (KV Windows & Doors specifically pays 30 days after receipt).

Payment methods: Visa-on-file, EFT, e-transfer, and cheque (cheques limited to Dirty Deeds, otherwise switched to EFT) each have their own recording/filing steps — see Visa Reconciliation for the RBC/Visa matching process.

Payroll: hourly staff punch in/out at the warehouse, cards collected Mondays by Production; payroll processed before Friday (RBC Express is one day delayed) and entered in Sage. Salaried staff are paid two weeks pre-paid.

Take a Payment / Record a Payment Legacy — HCP

This doc is from 2023 and records payments in HCP (HouseCall Pro), not Builder Prime — the recording steps need re-confirming against current BP workflow.

By e-transfer: clients send to info@completewd.com → print transaction record → record in HCP + client file label + receivable binder → file one copy in binder, one in client file.

By cheque/cash/credit-debit: similar record-in-HCP + label + receivable binder + filing pattern, with cash going into the deposit pouch in the safe.

Recording in HCP (legacy steps): Invoice tab → Pay → enter amount → Other → Other Type of Payment → record type/reference # → confirm customer-emailed-receipt checkbox → Paid - Other → Finish.

Submit a Receipt Draft Legacy — HCP

Sign-out/sign-in booklet required for the Visa, Home Depot, and gas cards where they're stored — log every time a card is taken or returned.

Receipts must be brought in promptly with: your name; customer last name + HCP# if job-related; what was purchased; which trailer (if trailer-related); note if it's stock. Missing receipts cause job-breakdown issues at cash-out.

Cash Outs & Job Pouch

Preparing a completed job for cash-out, and daily job-pouch logging.

Preparing Folders for Cash Outs

Trigger: job is fully installed and paid in full.

Collect invoices: match paid invoices (top of filing cabinet) to the job folder in "Waiting for Invoice" using PO, contract #, or customer last name.

Assemble folder: Job Cost Breakdown, Signed Contract, Chargeback Sheet (if any), Remeasure, Paid Invoices, Subcontractor Invoice (if any), Labour Breakdown, Work Order.

File remaining items in the job sleeve; discard packing slips and extra DaVinci quote copies.

Missing docs: pull Job Cost Breakdown / Labour Breakdown / Work Order from Office Team Documents → Admin Management → Admin Entries (job year).

Don't send to Job Costing if the job sleeve or any required invoice is missing.

Job Pouch Submission & Logging

Complete job pouches/paperwork neatly after every job (signed completion form, all receipts) and place on the shop bench for Production review.

Log all client notes/conversations and payment status (collected / not collected) in Builder Prime — for multi-day jobs, log daily, not just at completion.

This logging is the installer's own responsibility, not Production's — ask a teammate or the office if unsure how to log in BP.
