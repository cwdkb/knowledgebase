Marketing & Lead Source

Prepared by Kate Alsaybar · Source: Lead Source & Monthly Spend SOP / Checklist, SharePoint (last updated May 15, 2026)

Overview

To document all active and historical lead sources used by Complete Windows & Doors, identify which are paid or unpaid, explain who manages each, and outline how monthly advertising spend must be tracked in Builder Prime for accurate cost-per-lead reporting and budgeting.

Accessing Lead Source Tracking in Builder Prime

Navigation Path

Go to: Admin → Configure → Client Settings → Lead Sources

Within this section:

1. Select the lead source

2. Under Track Monthly Spend

3. Select Class: Unclassified (New Company)

4. Ensure the correct year is selected

5. Input the monthly spend amounts

Monthly Spend Tracking

Key rules for keeping cost-per-lead reporting and ROI analysis accurate.

Key Rules

All paid advertising sources must have monthly spend entered.

Tracking must be updated consistently throughout the year.

Most annual contracts or yearly totals should be divided evenly across 12 months and entered monthly for accurate reporting.

If costs fluctuate seasonally, actual monthly amounts may be entered instead.

Accurate tracking is required for: cost-per-lead reporting, budget planning, ROI analysis, and comparing lead source performance.

Lead Source Details

Full write-up per source — description, notes, and optimization angle.

1. Google Paid Search

Managed by: Gooder Marketing – Ryan Tomlinson.

Sponsored Google search ads for window/door company and replacement searches. One of the company's largest advertising budgets; leads come directly into Builder Prime.

To be verified: exact ad structure, current monthly spend, difference between Google Paid Search and Online Discovery.

Optimizable: SEO improvements, ad optimization, landing page improvements, keyword research.

Google LSA (Local Service Ads)

Google Local Service Ads, separate from standard Google Ads. Payment generally based on lead generation, customer interaction, and click/contact activity. Must track monthly charges.

Optimizable: review lead quality, optimize reviews and ratings, improve profile performance.

Online Discovery

Managed by: Gooder Marketing – Ryan Tomlinson.

Likely a separate Google or display-based advertising category.

2. Home Shows (Bradford Green, Orillia, Wasaga)

Trade shows and home renovation expos attended annually. Historical/discontinued: Barrie Fall Show, Sadlon Arena Show, Muskoka Show, Ribfest-related show.

Responsibilities before: booth booking, payment processing, prize/giveaway setup, brochure prep, display/signage, sweepstakes setup.

During: lead collection, client engagement, giveaway management.

After: lead entry, follow-up tracking, ROI review.

Bradford Green is the largest annual show; Orillia performs well due to premium booth location; Wasaga produced strong lead volume.

Optimizable: better giveaways, booth presentation, follow-up automation, improved lead capture.

3. Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram Ads)

Managed by: CGF Media – Cael Firth.

Paid social campaigns on Facebook and Instagram. One of the company's largest advertising budgets — separate from organic leads, must track monthly spend carefully.

Optimizable: ad creatives, video content, retargeting, audience testing, campaign optimization.

4. Facebook & Instagram Organic

Managed by: Complete WD + LADR Consulting – Sonia Dibias.

Leads from direct messages, organic posts, reels/videos, non-paid engagement. Does not require ad spend.

Optimizable: content strategy, consistent posting, engagement, before/after photos, video content.

5. Website

Managed by: Media Suite – Trevor Howard.

Leads submitted directly through the company website; feeds directly into Builder Prime. Maintenance handled externally and internally.

Optimizable: SEO, landing pages, conversion improvements, FAQ content, blog content.

6. ChatGPT / AI Search Discovery

Potential lead generation through AI search tools (ChatGPT, AI-generated search results, question-based searches).

Key strategy: add informational/FAQ-style content to the website backend to improve AI discoverability — homeowner questions, renovation guidance, buying advice, quote comparisons.

High potential — future focus area.

7. Reddit

Potential lead discovery through online discussions and community recommendations.

Research: local subreddit engagement, SEO indexing, brand mentions.

8. Yelp

Primarily organic. Company may have previously paid for Yelp advertising — verify if account is still active.

9. Nextdoor App

Local community platform for recommendations and neighborhood discussions.

Action required — research: existing company presence, advertising options, lead potential.

10. Billboards & City Bus

Managed by: Patterson Outdoor.

Outdoor billboard advertising — large advertising expense, includes highway and city billboard placements and city bus.

Optimizable: limited — mostly placement and design optimization.

11. Radio Advertising

Previous stations: Fresh 93.1, Rock 95.

Historically a very large advertising budget; Cottage Country Traffic sponsorships used seasonally. Currently paused/reduced.

If resumed: seasonal targeting, demographic analysis, ROI review.

12. Flyers / Printouts / Mail Outs

Includes flyers, postcards, print materials, business cards, Canada Post mail campaigns.

Research: Canada Post direct mail pricing, area targeting costs, postal walk segmentation.

Potential target areas: Barrie, Midhurst, Alliston, Orillia.

13. Lawn Signs

Supplier: C3R (Toronto).

Jobsite lawn signs for neighborhood exposure — larger-than-standard signage, may occasionally include customer incentives.

Optimizable: placement strategy, incentive programs, neighborhood targeting.

14. Trucks & Trailers

Vehicle decals and branded trailer advertising — truck decals, trailer wraps, van graphics.

Large one-time costs should typically be divided across the year and entered monthly.

15. Referrals

Customer referral program. Costs: referral gift cards, referral incentives.

16. Installer Referrals

Leads referred by installers. May include referral bonuses / incentive payments.

17. Repeat Customers

Returning customers completing additional work. No advertising spend required.

18. Self-Generated Canvassing

Door-to-door canvassing and neighborhood outreach.

Best times: slower seasons — late winter, early spring, potentially August if slow.

19. Shows & Events

Events outside traditional home shows — community fairs, festivals, vendor events.

Research: booth pricing, sponsorship requirements, attendance, vendor eligibility.

20. Insurance Companies

Referral/relationship based. Leads connected to insurance restoration or related projects. Generally not a direct advertising expense.

21. Property Management Companies

Referral/relationship based. Leads or projects connected through property management relationships — not advertising-based.
