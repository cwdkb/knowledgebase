Main Pipeline

New Lead – 1st Touch

Movement: Auto.

Entry: New lead created — exact source not yet confirmed.

Automations: "Lead Received Email – 1st Touch" (email), "1st Touch Text to Client" (SMS), plus 2 Create To-Do actions.

Confirmed: Serge confirmed on the July 21 call — these automations are scoped to only trigger for leads pushed in via API/Zapier integration. A lead entered manually (phone call, walk-in) does not trigger this automation set.

Speed to Lead 2 / 3 / 4

Movement: Manual moves, with automated texts (Touch 2–3) and text + email (Touch 4).

Entry: Advancing from the previous touch — no auto-advance rule found from touch 1→2 or 2→3.

Automations: Touch 2 sends SMS. Touch 3 sends SMS + email. Touch 4 sends SMS + email and updates the lead status — the only auto-advance rule found in this sequence.

Confirmed: Touch 4's "Update Lead Status" action fires after a 3-day delay and sets the lead to Lead Not Set – Rehash — verified directly in Admin → Configure → Automations, July 20.

Lead Set

Movement: Auto (can be manual if needed).

Entry: Appointment set (reporting milestone).

Automations: Create To-Do ("Lead Set – Not Confirmed Yet"), SMS notification to the sales rep.

Confirmed: no automation moves a lead from Lead Set into Lead Confirmed & Issued — only a Create To-Do and a rep SMS notification fire off Lead Set. The move into Lead Confirmed & Issued is a fully manual rep action.

Confirmed: Serge walked through this on the July 21 call — creating the meeting in the client profile auto-sets Lead Set; the assigned rep gets the automated confirmation text, and once they mark it confirmed the CRM auto-updates to Lead Confirmed & Issued. Appointments are usually set by Seth or Tristan, sometimes Tanya — whoever set it puts their name in the "Lead Set By" field.

Lead Confirmed & Issued

Movement: Manual — no automation triggers this transition (see the Lead Set card above).

Entry: Lead/appointment confirmed (reporting milestone: "Lead Issued").

Automations: Email to client, Create To-Do to send brochures, and SMS appointment confirmation — all fire 1 minute after status is set. A reminder SMS also fires 1 hour before the meeting.

Manual action: rep must send the brochures per the to-do.

Confirmed: Serge explained on the July 17 call — starting a project/estimate under the client profile auto-moves the lead to Demo. If the rep marks the meeting result as "No Show," it moves to No Show/Reset. If they meet but the job is outside scope, that's No Demo (no quote issued). All three are manual rep actions/decisions, not separate automation triggers.

Demo

Movement: Auto.

Entry: Confirmed — starting a project/estimate under the client profile auto-moves the lead here from Lead Confirmed & Issued (reporting milestone: "Demo").

Automations: none found directly tied to this lead status. A related meeting-type automation ("Experience After Estimate") fires by meeting type — In-Home Estimate.

Open question: whether that meeting-type automation is the real trigger here, and which outcome routes to Estimate Sent, Demo/No Sale – Rehash, or No Demo.

Estimate Sent – 3 Nudges

Movement: Auto.

Entry: Estimate sent to client.

Automations: Immediate SMS follow-up, then Sales Nudges 1–3 by SMS and Nudges 1–2 by email, 1 day after status is set.

Confirmed: auto-advances to Demo/No Sale – Rehash after 31 days of inactivity if not sold; otherwise manual advance to Job Sold or Pending/Follow Up.

Open question: whether sales reps are also calling between nudges.

Confirmed: Serge stated live on the July 17 call that this bucket is "3 Nudges" — matching the automation config (3 SMS + 2 email). The Bucket Cheat Sheet ("5 Nudges") and the Serge-version status list ("4 Nudges") both look stale and likely just need a documentation fix.

E-Signature Sent

Movement: Auto.

Entry: E-signature document sent to client.

Automations: Immediate SMS follow-up from the sales rep, plus a Create To-Do for the rep when the e-sig is about to expire.

Confirmed: auto-advances to Pending if the e-signature expires unsigned.

Confirmed: Serge confirmed on the July 21 call — the signed path is a manual checkpoint. Once the client e-signs, an email goes out to the sales rep to co-sign; once the rep signs, the CRM sends the signed copy to both the client and the company, which is what flags it as an official sold contract.

Salesrep to Sign

Internal checkpoint — the salesperson needs to sign off after the e-signature comes in from the client, before it becomes an official sale.

Confirmed: Serge confirmed on the July 21 call — client signs first, then an automated email goes to the sales rep to co-sign. Once the rep signs, the CRM sends the signed copy to the client and the company, flagging it as a sold contract.

Incomplete Install

Installation started but not finished — needs follow-up before it can move to invoicing. Separate from "Install In Progress" above, which is still actively underway.

Confirmed: Serge confirmed on the July 21 call this is a fully manual flow, no automations. Front desk invoices for the completed portion (holding back the remainder), the job moves back to Job Incomplete so production can finish it, and once complete it moves to Invoice Job Complete to bill the held-back balance.

Invoice (Complete / Incomplete) → Paid/Cash Out

Invoice: separate Create To-Do actions for "Job Incomplete" and "Job Complete," plus invoice reminder emails to the client and a completion notice to the sales rep.

Paid/Cash Out: cash-out to-dos to the PM (ASAP), Marie, and Serge, plus a thank-you/gift-card email and a review-request SMS.

Confirmed: Serge walked through the process on the July 21 call — the Project Manager gets the first cash-out review to-do, then about a week later Marie and Serge get a follow-up review to-do; once they approve it, the job moves to Job Closed. He didn't correct the "1 week" vs. "2 weeks" labels directly, so that wording may still be stale, but the process and approval chain are confirmed.

To-Dos Process

Review Cadence

Every staff member reviews their open To-Dos twice a day — morning and afternoon. Overdue and due-today items sort to the top.

To-Dos vs. Call Queue

All customer contact tasks (calls, follow-ups, reminders) are created as To-Dos — never as Call Queue entries. Call Queue is front-desk-only, for general call logging.

Naming Convention

Label call-related To-Dos as: "📞 Call [Customer Name] – [Purpose]"

Completing a To-Do

Check the box → Save and Close, and add or update a note under the client profile summarizing the outcome. If a follow-up is still needed, update the due date instead of closing it out.

Who's Responsible

Sales and project managers create customer-related To-Dos for every follow-up, call, and next step. Front desk and admin use the Call Queue only for general call tracking — not customer To-Dos. Management reviews compliance and makes sure overdue tasks actually get resolved.

Setting Outcomes on a Call

Voicemail left → push the due date to the next follow-up date. Contact made and resolved → add a note under the client profile, then check the box → Save and Close.

Scheduling & Timing

To-Dos can be date-specific, but they don't need to be time-specific unless timing is actually critical — e.g. a confirmed callback appointment.

Maintenance Cadence

Daily: review the dashboard, clear anything due or overdue. ASAP: resolve any overdue To-Do — don't let it sit. Weekly: clear out old, completed, or duplicate entries.

Lead Not Set – Rehash: the L-A-D-R Process

L — Locate

Find leads inactive 1–2 months or longer in the Rehash bucket that have already received all automated follow-ups.

A — Assess

Confirm no estimate was scheduled, automations have already gone out, and there's been no recent customer response. The goal is to figure out which of these is true: the project was completed elsewhere, it's postponed, or they're still planning and may need our help.

D — Do

Call first, then follow up by text if no answer. If they're not ready, ask what's holding them back and when to follow up again, and create a To-Do for that date.

Voicemail script: "Hi [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] calling from Complete Windows & Doors. I'm just reaching out to check in on the project you had inquired about with us. I wanted to see if this is something we can still help you with, or if you've already completed the project or decided to move in another direction. If you could give us a quick call back and let us know, that would be great. You can reach us at [office number]. I will also send you a text message, so feel free to reply there as well and let us know the status of your project. Thanks and have a great [day/evening]."

Follow-up text script: "Hi [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from Complete Windows & Doors. I'm just checking in to see if texting is easier for you. Are you still moving forward with your project, and if so, what material would you like a quote for?"

R — Record

Note what the voicemail or text actually covered — not just "Called and left voicemail."

Example note: "Called and left voicemail checking on the status of their project and whether they still require our help. Advised that a follow-up text message was also sent and asked them to reply by phone or text with an update."

Glossary — Team Terms & Shortcuts

Vocabulary specific to how this org talks about the pipeline — for anyone new to the team.

Bucket Cheat Sheet

Tristyn's reference doc mapping Builder Prime's internal statuses to plain-English "bucket" names — the canonical naming source for this page.

Speed to Lead

The automated multi-touch follow-up sequence (Touches 2–4) that runs right after a new lead comes in, before an appointment is set.

Nudges

The automated SMS/email reminders sent after an estimate goes out. Currently 3 SMS + 2 email — confirmed live by Serge on the July 17 call.

Rehash

Reactivating a lead that's gone cold after all its automated follow-ups have run out. See the L-A-D-R process.

L-A-D-R

Locate, Assess, Do, Record — the 4-step manual process for rehashing a cold lead.

To-Dos vs. Call Queue

To-Dos are for every customer-contact task. Call Queue is front-desk-only general call logging — the two are never meant to mix.

Cash Out

The final payment/commission release after a job is invoiced and paid — split into separate PM, Marie, and Serge to-dos with different delay windows.

Job Sold vs. Job Sold/Deposit Required

Two different status fields (lead status vs. project status) — confirmed they don't fire together automatically; a rep sets both manually.

Job Cancelled/Declined vs. Job Sold – Canceled

Both were real statuses that Serge recently merged into "Job Cancelled/Declined" — that's the only one left in the live lead list.

Not Yet Audited

This status wasn't covered in the live automation-config review call — nothing's confirmed to fire (or not fire) here yet.
