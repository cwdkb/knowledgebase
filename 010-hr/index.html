HR Manager — Role & Responsibilities

Core areas: Recruitment & Staffing, Employee Relations & Engagement, HR Policies & Compliance, Compensation & Benefits, Training & Development, Performance Management, and Strategic HR & Reporting.

In a window & door supply/install company specifically: recruiting skilled installers, sales staff, and office personnel; ensuring compliance with health & safety standards on job sites; managing seasonal hiring and turnover; supporting training on customer service, safety, and technical skills.

Daily: respond to employee inquiries (payroll, policies, vacation, benefits), support managers with employee relations issues, maintain HR files, monitor attendance/vacation/leaves, coordinate interviews or onboarding sessions.

Health & Safety Manager — Role & Responsibilities

Core areas: Policy & Compliance, Risk Management & Hazard Control, Training & Awareness, Emergency Preparedness, Reporting & Documentation, and Collaboration — including supporting HR in return-to-work programs after an injury.

Key hazards for this business: handling heavy doors/windows, working with glass, ladders/scaffolding, power tools, and driving company vehicles.

Daily/weekly: walk warehouse/yard safety checks, monitor PPE compliance, jobsite inspections with project/production managers, toolbox talks, equipment checks (ladders, lifts, harnesses).

Overtime Hours Banking Policy 2026.05.29

Purpose: voluntary program giving employees scheduling flexibility while helping manage seasonal workload and payroll predictability. Participation requires a signed authorization form and management approval — without it, overtime is simply paid out on the regular cycle.

Overtime definition: hours worked beyond 44/week.

Banking window: the week after Labour Day (September) through March 31. Overtime worked April 1–early September is not eligible for banking and is paid out normally.

Banking cap: 88 hours. Anything above that is automatically paid out.

Using banked hours: between October 1 and May 31, via the approved Microsoft Teams request form, subject to management approval and operational needs.

Payout of unused hours: begins May 1, processed incrementally through payroll, fully paid out by May 31 — no carryover without management approval. Banking eligibility pauses each April 1 until the next Labour Day.

Workplace Relationship & Related Conflict of Interests Policy 2026.01.05

Standalone, more current version of Handbook section 2.6. Applies to all employees, contractors, managers, and executives across all departments. Does not regulate personal lives — only where a personal relationship intersects with the workplace.

Disclosure required only when a relationship creates or could be perceived to create: a reporting/supervisory relationship, influence over pay/scheduling/discipline/reviews/promotion/assignment, access to confidential info that could be misused, or a risk of favoritism/retaliation/disruption. Disclosure is confidential and non-disciplinary in itself.

Company response is preventative, not punitive: may include adjusting reporting lines, reassigning duties, or adding confidentiality safeguards.

What is disciplinable: failing to disclose a required conflict, favoritism/misuse of authority, breach of confidentiality, harassment/coercion/retaliation, or disruptive conduct.

Company Credit Card, Purchasing Card & Fuel Card Policy 2026-06-24

Authorized: job-site materials/supplies, emergency purchases, approved office supplies, fuel (company vehicles and, when authorized, personal vehicles on company business), other management-approved items.

Prohibited: personal purchases, cash advances, anything unrelated to company business or not pre-authorized.

Documentation: itemized receipt for every purchase, job-site address on the receipt where applicable, team lead's name recorded for fuel purchases, all receipts submitted to the office at least weekly. Lost/stolen/damaged cards reported to the Office Manager immediately.

Mobile Phone & Electronic Device Policy 2026.03.26

Applies to field staff and office staff, at job sites, the office, company vehicles, and remote/off-site work.

Permitted: work-related communication/CRM/scheduling use, job documentation photos/video, approved apps, plus personal use during emergencies or scheduled breaks.

Prohibited: personal use during active work time, excessive texting/social media/personal calls.

Driving: handheld device use while driving is strictly prohibited; hands-free only where legal and safe; pull over if a call is necessary.

Safety: device use prohibited while operating tools/machinery, working at heights, installing, or handling materials.

GPS: company vehicles may carry GPS tracking for scheduling/routing/safety/asset protection — company-owned vehicles only, not personal devices.

Dress Code & Personal Hygiene Policy Work in Progress

Filed under the generic name "Work in Progress.docx" — treat as an unfinished, unconfirmed draft, not a finalized policy, even though it carries a March 26, 2026 effective date on its own cover page.

General: clean/neat/professional appearance appropriate to role, basic hygiene (bathing, deodorant, clean clothing daily, oral hygiene) — addressed privately and with accommodation consideration if disruptive.

Installers/field staff: CSA-approved safety footwear mandatory, durable work pants, company-issued/plain shirts; no jogging/sweatpants, tank tops, or open-toed footwear.

Production/office staff: business casual — clean jeans/dress pants, polos/sweaters/casual tops, presentable footwear; no excessively ripped or graphic-offensive clothing.

Front desk/customer-facing: business casual required at all times — blouses/polos/dress shirts, dress pants/skirts/professional jeans; no athletic wear or revealing clothing.

Enforcement: verbal reminder → requirement to change attire → written warning for repeat non-compliance → further discipline. Accommodation available for medical/religious/protected-ground needs.
