Production Buckets & Job Flow

From the Production Meeting agenda/minutes — the mandatory setup steps before a job can move into production, plus the ongoing cash flow and documentation rules production runs on.

Mandatory: PM + Remeasure Tech Assigned Before Production

Every job must have a Project Manager assigned and a Remeasure Technician assigned before it moves into production. A new pre-production bucket was added specifically to enforce this — its purpose is to make sure all setup steps are completed before the job moves forward.

Key rule: No job advances without (1) PM assigned, (2) Remeasure tech assigned, and (3) the pre-production bucket marked complete.

Final production bucket list, the purpose of each bucket, and "done" criteria for each were still being locked down as of the source meeting — confirm current bucket list with Serge.

Cash-Outs & Invoice Tagging

2-week cash-out rule: Cash-outs must be completed no more than 2 weeks after a project is completed and paid — flagged as an immediate priority since cash-outs were behind and affecting financial clarity.

Invoice tagging (mandatory): Tag every invoice with the customer's last name plus project number, e.g. "Smith 12345" — not first name only. The goal is that anyone in the office can identify which job an invoice belongs to without asking around.

Before & After Pictures (CompanyCam)

Set up the project in CompanyCam through Builder Prime at the time the remeasure is booked. The RM tech takes the "before" pictures; installers take the "after" pictures from the exact same spot the befores were taken. Installers post both to the Installer group with the project name; Nev and Trystin pull them from there for social media marketing.

Inspection Fees

Inspection fees must be discussed with the customer before booking the appointment, and invoiced immediately upon booking — not after the visit. The employee attending the inspection must provide a detailed report as soon as possible. If the customer goes on to purchase a new product, the inspection/service fee is credited toward that order.

Remeasure Accuracy KPI

Production Department KPIs track Remeasure Technician accuracy — the objective is to reduce remeasures, remakes, and RM errors. Errors are reported via a Microsoft Form and tracked against a bonus allowance.

Remeasure Process (Post-Upload)

What happens once the Remeasure Technician's measurements are back — two scenarios depending on whether a change order is triggered.

Scenario 1 — No Change Order Needed

1. RM Tech confirms all measurements and notes are complete and accurate. 2. Admin uploads the Remeasure (RM) to Builder Prime. 3. Admin updates the job status to "Needs to be Ordered and Costed." 4. Admin places the job file in the "Needs to be Ordered" tray in the production department.

Scenario 2 — Change Order Needed

1. RM Tech confirms measurements/notes are complete and marks the pouch with a change-order-needed indicator. 2. Job status moves to "Contract on Hold." 3. Builder Prime automatically creates a To-Do notifying the sales rep a change order is needed; the RM tech updates that To-Do with accurate notes. 4. RM Tech uploads the RM to BP. 5. Sales Rep runs the change order process, then checks/initials on the pouch that it's done. 6. Production re-writes the RM if needed, then returns the job file to Admin once the change order is complete. 7. Admin updates all job info and re-uploads the RM to BP if needed.

Formula & Final Pouch Checklist

Total Install Time = Base Window & Door Time + Drive Time + 1 Hour (Lunch/Load/Unload) + Adjustment Time (difficulty/access/extras). Always round up when scheduling, and write extra hours separately from the base total on the pouch — e.g. "Base Windows: 10 hrs, Extras (Access & Tear-Out): +6 hrs, Total: 16 hrs." Do not write only the final total.

Before submitting the pouch: ☐ Drive time accounted for ☐ Load/unload + lunch included ☐ Correct baseline per opening type ☐ Bay/patio additions reviewed ☐ Access & tear-out difficulty reviewed ☐ Capping/brick mold time included ☐ Extras noted (headers, cutouts, stone, shutters, jambs) ☐ 3rd man noted if needed ☐ Total hours clearly documented.

Change Order Process

Change orders can surface at remeasure, during production planning, at install, or in post-install review — before the final invoice goes out. The rule that matters most: a change order must be created the moment an issue or extra is identified, and the final invoice cannot be sent until it's processed.

Priority Rule — No Change Order, No Final Invoice

A Change Order cannot be created after the final invoice has been sent or paid — no exceptions. The moment an installer, coordinator, or sales rep becomes aware of a scope change or extra, it must be documented, created in Builder Prime, and sent to the client to approve and sign before any additional work proceeds or the final invoice goes out.

Change Order vs. Additional Scope
TypeDescriptionExample
Change OrderRevises an existing element or pricing on the same contractDoor color changed black to red, added flashing, minor cost adjustment
Additional ScopeAdds a new section of work to an already-sold contractAdding siding/enclosure work discovered at remeasure, a secondary project phase
Roles

Salesperson: determines Change Order vs. Additional Scope, creates/prices it, sends for client e-signature, notifies front desk once sent, confirms deposit requirements and signs once client approval is received.

Front Desk: tracks unsigned or unpaid items and follows up; attaches signed documents to the original contract and sets status to "Needs to be Ordered and Costed."

Production: reviews the "Needs to be Ordered and Costed" bucket for new/revised contracts and ensures all scopes tied to the same contract are ordered together.

Deposits & Timeline

Small changes (under $1,000): no additional deposit required — clear the deposit field before generating. Major changes ($1,000+): deposit required; job stays on "Deposit Required" until it's received. Finance deals: combine all scopes into one contract and confirm the customer's approval covers the full contract value.

Change orders/additional scopes must be created and sent to the client within 24 hours of the remeasure or discovery, and fully signed and filed before the job moves to "Needs to be Ordered and Costed." If a change order sits awaiting signature more than a week, front desk or sales follows up. Signed change orders are stapled/merged with the original contract for audit tracking.
